abstract: "The choice of operator tracking technology plays a critical role in addressing the unique challenges faced in manual assembly, from high-accuracy localization to scalability, operator acceptance and environmental adaptability. This paper compares four indoor Real-Time Localization Systems: Ultra-Wideband, Bluetooth Low Energy , Acoustic, and Vision AI. These technologies are evaluated across key parameters, including precision, latency, robustness, scalability and user experience in an industrially relevant environment. The analysis identifies the strengths and limitations of each system, supporting decision-making in selecting effective tracking solutions for specific needs."
