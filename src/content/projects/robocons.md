## Why

Construction struggles with labour shortages, safety risks and project delays. ROBOCONS aims to design the safe, efficient and sustainable job sites of tomorrow by bringing together multi-robot orchestration, cognitive algorithms, tactile exoskeletons and digital twins.

## Flanders Make's part

Flanders Make is responsible for the **P2 AR prototype**, one of the project's five prototypes, and for the **HMI and human–robot collaboration (HRC)** work. We collaborate on this with the other European RTOs and technology providers in the consortium.
